## What data we collect

When you visit this static website, certain technical and usage data are automatically collected indirectly via *Google Analytics* to help us understand how visitors interact with our site (**static personal blog**). This includes:

- **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, time spent on pages, click patterns, and referral sources (how you arrived at our site).
- **Device & Technical Information:** IP address (anonymized/masked), browser type, operating system, device type (mobile, desktop, tablet), and screen resolution.
- **Approximate Location Data:** Country, region, or city level inferred from your anonymized IP address.

This site is static and does not collect personal data directly through user accounts, comments, or form submissions.

## Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies provided by **Google Analytics** to gather usage metrics and traffic insights. 

A cookie-consent banner is presented upon your first visit. Google Analytics scripts will only set cookies and process tracking data if you explicitly choose to accept analytics cookies through this banner. You can change or revoke your consent at any time via the consent banner link in the site footer.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data protection laws, you have specific rights regarding your data:

- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw your consent for analytics cookies at any time using the consent banner on this website or by clearing your browser cookies.
- **Data Subject Rights:** You have the right to request information regarding whether personal data is processed, as well as the rights to access, delete, or restrict the processing of your data where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
